# Pluck Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. This policy covers Pluck for Mac.

**Pluck collects no data.** It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code
that reports anything. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or anyone else. The App Store version
makes no network requests of its own.

## What stays on your Mac

- **The PDFs you open** are read on your Mac to pull out their images, text and stat blocks. They are never
  uploaded. Pluck saves only what you export, where you choose to put it.
- **Your settings**, such as the WebP quality and the text options, and **recent files**, are saved on your Mac.
  Open Recent's Clear Menu forgets the files.

Deleting the app from your Mac leaves this behind: macOS keeps an app's saved data in the Containers folder of
your Library folder until you delete the folder named Pluck there.

## Other apps on your Mac

Pluck can ask Scrollkeeper, a separate app, for the list of PDFs you own and to download one, and can send
images to Mapsmith. Those requests go from app to app on your Mac, through a folder the apps share;
Scrollkeeper's own policy covers what it does with them.

## The disk image version

The version downloaded from GitHub can check GitHub for a newer release when it opens, if you leave that setting
on. The check sends GitHub the same request any web browser would; the developer sees nothing of it.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may send crash reports for
Pluck to the developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data from the app.

## Children

Pluck collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here, with a new effective date. Questions go to the
